What we treat

Sports injuries chiropractic treatment

Whether you run Lion's Head, surf, cycle or lift, an injury can stop you in your tracks. We work out what went wrong, treat it with hands-on care, and help you get back to training safely, and stronger.

Does this sound familiar?

Sports injuries can show up in different ways. You might notice:

  • Pain that starts during or after training
  • A niggle that keeps coming back every time you increase your load
  • Stiffness or tightness that limits your performance
  • Swelling, tenderness or bruising after a knock or twist
  • Pain in a joint such as your knee, ankle, hip or shoulder
  • Feeling weaker or less stable on one side

We'll look at how the injury happened and how you move, so we can treat the cause and help stop it coming back.

What causes sports injuries?

Most sports injuries come from load the body wasn't ready for, rather than bad luck. Common reasons include:

Doing too much too soon

Ramping up distance, weight or training days faster than your body can adapt.

Repetitive movements

Running, cycling and swimming repeat the same motion thousands of times.

Skipping the warm-up

Cold muscles and joints are less ready for sudden effort.

Falls and knocks

Contact, tumbles and twisted ankles on trails or in the surf.

Stiff joints

A stiff joint makes the muscles and joints around it work harder.

Footwear and technique

Worn-out shoes or small technique faults add up over time.

How we treat it

Every treatment starts with listening to your full story and assessing how the injured area and the joints around it move. Your treatment is then tailored to you and may combine:

Chiropractic adjustments

Gentle adjustments to restore movement in stiff joints. The pop is optional.

Mobilisations

Slower, gentle movements to restore range of motion after an injury.

Dry needling

Releases tight, overworked muscles and trigger points.

Kinesio taping

Supports injured muscles and joints so you can keep moving while they heal.

Massage gun therapy

Percussive therapy to aid recovery in tight, tired muscles.

Advice for home

Rehab exercises and a plan to return to your sport safely.

Sports injury questions

How many sessions will I need?

It depends on the type of injury, how long you've had it and your goals. We'll talk you through a plan at your first visit and adjust it as you improve.

Do I have to stop training?

Not always. Where possible we'll help you modify your training rather than stop completely, and give you a plan to build back up safely.

I'm nervous about being adjusted. Is there another option?

Yes. Adjustments are only one option. Mobilisations, dry needling, taping and soft tissue work can all be used instead, and nothing is done without your consent.

Can I claim from my medical aid?

You can claim for your sessions from your medical aid. You pay at your session and we email you an invoice to submit to your scheme. Our fees are below standard medical aid rates.
